A classic SaaS homepage — hero, three pricing cards, dashboard screenshot — would misrepresent Roncyo. We are not selling one subscription dashboard. We are a studio that keeps creating small products.

## What visitors should feel

Visitors should feel closer to a design atelier or an indie lab than to an enterprise software pitch. Brand first. Products as a gallery. Philosophy in plain language.

## Who earns revenue

Money is made by DestCard, RonFax, tools, and apps. Roncyo holds the portfolio together.
