DestCard started as a focused English product page — not a company brochure. Within nine days, Search Console showed a few hundred impressions. That is not a business yet, but it is a signal: Google will give a clean, useful page a chance.

## What we copied into the studio model

The lesson for Roncyo is not “buy a hundred domains tomorrow.” It is: copy the launch loop. Ship a small product, measure impressions and clicks, then decide whether it deserves a deeper investment or an independent domain.

## Role of the mother brand

The mother brand exists so those experiments have a shared home, shared legal entity, and shared trust — while winners graduate when they earn it.
