Most tiny tools will stay tiny. That is fine. A studio model accepts a high experiment rate and a low promotion rate for winners.

## Boring metrics on purpose

The useful metrics are boring on purpose: impressions, clicks, signups, payments. Aesthetic taste gets you noticed; retention and revenue decide what graduates.

## Compounding assets

Over years, the asset is not a hundred disconnected sites. It is a recognizable studio plus a handful of products that compound.
